### 4. Community Pulse
The community is currently deep in the "infrastructure phase" of AI. Across Dev.to and Lobste.rs, the consensus is that the era of "just throw a prompt at it" is over. 

**Common Themes:**
*   **Cost vs. Validation:** There is a strong movement toward model routing and local evaluation tools to avoid the "GPT-6 bill shock."
*   **The "Agentic Gap":** Developers are expressing frustration with agent reliability. The recurring pattern is a transition from trusting agent output to building validation pipelines (RAG-critics, model routers, and custom test harnesses).
*   **Security Concerns:** We are seeing a spike in discussions around prompt injection, lateral movement through agent tool access (like `.env` file exposure), and the risks associated with giving AI models network or filesystem privileges.
*   **Local-First Trend:** The "Slow and Steady" Hacktoberfest challenges on Dev.to demonstrate a clear interest in building local-first tools (e.g., SlothOps, DownloadsSloth) that prioritize privacy and offline performance over massive, opaque, cloud-reliant models.
