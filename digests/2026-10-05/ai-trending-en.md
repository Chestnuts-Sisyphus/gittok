## 3. Trend Signal Analysis
The data indicates a massive shift from **Generative UI** (creating images/video) toward **Agent Orchestration** and **Cost Efficiency**. While "Agent-Reach" demonstrates the hunger for multi-modal, web-browsing agents, projects like "caveman" and "ECC" address the economic reality of running these agents.

We are seeing the emergence of the **"Agent Harness"** as a distinct category of software—infrastructure specifically designed to patch the weaknesses of LLMs (hallucination, cost, context loss). This is a direct response to the saturation of general-purpose models; developers are now looking for tools to make *existing* models smarter and cheaper to run. Additionally, the rise of local inference engines (like ds4) suggests a "decentralization" trend where users prefer running models on their own hardware rather than relying on centralized APIs.
